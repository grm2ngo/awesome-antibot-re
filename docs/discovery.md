# Discovery channels

These are search starting points, not an accepted-resource list. Specific articles/posts must pass [the evidence gates](../CURATION.md#mandatory-gates). See [the multilingual scout playbook](agents/social-regional.md) for queries and provenance checks.

## X

Follow original researchers and technical threads to their public code, papers and long-form explanations. Store exact post URLs when inspected. In this review, the author-linked [veritas profile](https://x.com/blastbots) could not be opened; no specific X post was accepted. A profile is a lead, not a technical resource.

## CSDN

Search original Chinese technical articles and trace reprints back to authors. Distinguish publication dates from recommendations and crawl timestamps. The [Chaoxing slider analysis](https://blog.csdn.net/BI__NG/article/details/148828536) is held for evidence review, not accepted: its full body was accessible, but date provenance and technical claims remain insufficiently established. See [the review packet](../reviews/2026-09-09.md).

## Other communities

- [Kanxue](https://bbs.kanxue.com/): discover exact technical threads rather than cite the homepage as research.
- [nullpt.rs](https://nullpt.rs/): author/blog discovery; accepted writeups link to specific articles in the README.
- Zhihu, Juejin and Habr: search technical explanations and follow original citations.
- Conference proceedings, author publication pages and code-linked slides: follow the [publications playbook](agents/articles.md).

Original-language posts can qualify on their own technical merits. Translations and reposts are useful for discovery but cannot independently corroborate their source. The company searches multiple languages each run and records coverage gaps without filling quotas.

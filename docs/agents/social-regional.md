Rotate aliases: TikTok/抖音/Douyin, reCAPTCHA/验证码, reverse engineering/逆向/реверс, VM/JSVMP/虚拟机. Aliases help search but do not prove products share implementations. Search syntax varies by engine; split poor Boolean queries.

## Inspection

Open canonical posts and complete relevant threads. Record author, permalink/post ID, original date versus update/recommendation date and linked artifacts. Snippets, reposts and screenshots alone remain leads.

For CSDN and other repost-heavy channels, inspect original/reprint labels, citations, code links, dates and overlapping paragraphs. Reverse-search distinctive phrases to find the original. Translations add access value but are not independent confirmation; preserve original terms where translation affects meaning.

For X, read the complete thread and technical artifact. An original evidence-rich post may qualify; engagement and a link to another article do not make it a separate research resource. Blocked/deleted/login-only posts stay leads unless a legitimate author-published public copy can be read.

Deliver evidence records, provenance chains, held leads, exact searches and access failures. There is no per-platform addition quota.

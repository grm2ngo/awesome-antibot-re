## Execution and debate

Use the waves in [the staffing plan](company.md#staffing-and-cadence). Assign a Gatekeeper who did not propose the candidate. Self-review never counts. Give the verifier actual evidence records and source links.

Round 1: verifier opens sources and states concrete gaps or alternative interpretations; another scout challenges provenance, novelty or scope. If no objection exists, record the supporting evidence rather than invent disagreement.

Round 2: proposers respond with evidence or narrower wording. The verifier marks objections resolved/partial/open. The chair preserves dissent and holds unsupported claims. Zero accepted additions is a valid result.

Keep additions, corrections/removals and new ideas separate. Each idea needs a problem, benefit, cost, strongest counterargument and smallest reversible trial. Ideas await manual approval.

## Search coverage and stopping

Assign product, technique, missing source type, language and known sources to exclude. Rotate lanes; cover English plus at least two relevant non-English languages each run. Try two materially different query forms per lane and follow a useful author/citation/code trail where available. Record exact queries, date filters, inspected results and access limits; coverage targets are not acceptance quotas.

Stop a query family after two different searches yield no new eligible originals. Follow at most two citation hops by default. Hold inaccessible leads after checking the canonical source and one legitimate author-linked public alternative. Carry useful unfinished leads forward. Use recent discovery and older foundational work.

Use [the evidence template](evidence-template.md). Admission rules live in CURATION.md and cannot be weakened to meet search targets.

# A small research company

Mission: maintain a concise, evidence-backed awesome anti-bot RE list. The maintainer controls publication and approves new initiatives.

| Position | Responsibility | Decision rights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maintainer / editor-in-chief | Chooses direction and reviews concrete changes and ideas | Final approval of publication and new initiatives |
| Managing editor / parent agent | Assigns work, reconciles evidence, maintains navigation and prepares review packets | Can narrow or hold a proposal; cannot waive evidence gates or self-approve publication |
| Expansion Lead / R&D subagent | Finds adjacent ideas, neglected products, languages and original-source communities | Proposes research assignments and ideas; cannot implement unapproved ideas |
| Repository researcher | Inspects code, fixtures, commits and fork changes | Recommends sources with evidence |
| Publications researcher | Inspects blogs, papers, talks and corrections | Recommends sources with evidence |
| Regional/social researcher | Searches X, CSDN, forums and multilingual author trails | Separates original sources, derivatives and leads |
| Gatekeeper / quality subagent | Independently reopens evidence and challenges scope, provenance, claims and conflicts | Blocks promotion of unsupported material; records exact evidence needed to release a hold |

## Staffing and cadence

Expansion Lead and Gatekeeper participate in **every** run. These are recurring roles, not background agents left running continuously. Reuse an agent only when it has not authored the claim it must independently verify.

With three child slots: wave A runs Expansion Lead and two scouts; wave B runs the remaining scout and Gatekeeper while completed scouts answer challenges as needed. The parent also performs editing and checks. Keep the same Gatekeeper responsible for the final packet; if it proposed an item, assign another independent reviewer for that item.

Runs occur every three days at 09:00 Asia/Saigon. Search English plus at least two relevant other languages each run, with rotating coverage. Coverage attempts are logged; language or platform quotas never force acceptance.

## Decision meeting

1. R&D proposes scope and scouts submit evidence independently.
2. Gatekeeper raises concrete objections and a scout argues the strongest alternative interpretation.
3. Proposer responds with evidence, narrower wording or withdrawal.
4. Managing editor records outcomes: ready for maintainer review, hold, rejected, or idea awaiting approval. Open material contradictions force hold.
5. Maintainer reviews a draft PR and a separate idea list. Silence, time elapsed, previous blanket authorization and an agent vote are not approval of a new revision.

Track useful outcomes: evidence coverage, resolved contradictions, distinct original contributions and maintained navigation. Raw link counts and unanimous votes are not quality metrics.

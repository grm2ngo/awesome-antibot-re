# Blog, paper and talk scout

Read [the policy](../../CURATION.md).

1. Search combinations such as `TikTok virtual machine reverse engineering`, `reCAPTCHA security analysis paper`, `Akamai sensor deobfuscation trace`, `JSVMP conference slides`. Vary mechanisms and synonyms beyond “bypass.”
2. Follow references, author publication pages, attached code/slides and corrections. Resolve search results to exact original articles, not homepages.
3. Read full relevant sections, methods and limitations. Record paper version and date provenance. For talks inspect transcript or specific slides/time ranges; titles and descriptions are insufficient.
4. Map mechanisms and measured results to artifacts or exact explanatory sections. Attribute benchmarks and restrict quantities/success claims to their evaluated setting.
5. Compare historical results to later corrections. Send evidence to the verifier; narrow wording if the original material supports less than the title suggests.

Deliver source/section references, queries, provenance trails and inaccessible items. Keep author directories distinct from accepted technical resources.
